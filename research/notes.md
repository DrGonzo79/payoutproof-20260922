## Daily idea

- **Date:** 2026-09-22
- **Email subject:** “Turn lowball offers on totaled cars into $$$”
- **Idea:** a fast, lower-cost check of a total-loss insurer valuation using current comparable vehicle listings.
- **Public idea:** https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/total-loss-payout-checker-for-totaled-cars

The newsletter says roughly 25% of crashed vehicles are declared total losses and frames the current alternative as an independent appraisal costing about $500 with a week-long wait. It proposes extracting year, make, model, trim, and mileage from an insurer valuation, comparing current listings nearby, and testing a $99 report. Those claims are hypotheses from the source, not independently verified facts in this prototype.

## Product interpretation

The strongest wedge is not “certified appraisal” — certification, jurisdictional rules, marketplace licensing, and professional liability require validation. The safer first product is an explainable **valuation audit** that helps a claimant find questionable inputs and draft a written request for clarification. Demo listings are fictional and no live marketplace integrations are implied.
